REDUCED RATE OF VAT ON DEMOLITION AND RECONSTRUCTION: works consisting of the demolition of a building and the reconstruction of a dwelling may qualify for the reduced rate of value added tax of six per cent in place of the standard rate of twenty-one per cent. With effect from 1 January 2024 the regime applying throughout Belgium is permanent and is subject to substantive conditions, namely that the dwelling is the sole and own dwelling of the person for whom the works are carried out, that its habitable surface does not exceed 200 square metres, that that person is domiciled there without delay, and that the conditions continue to be satisfied for a period of five years. With effect from 1 January 2025 the regime extends to demolition followed by reconstruction of a dwelling which is then let on a long-term basis to a natural person who establishes their main residence there, subject to the same limit of habitable surface. A DECLARATION MUST PRECEDE THE CHARGEABLE EVENT: application of the reduced rate requires that a declaration be lodged before the value added tax becomes chargeable. Where the declaration is lodged after amounts have been invoiced, the reduced rate is not available in respect of those amounts and the position cannot be corrected by re-invoicing. The company accordingly does not invoice at the reduced rate unless the required declaration is held, and the timing is addressed before the first invoice is issued rather than during the works. THE FIVE-YEAR CONDITION IS CONTINUING: the conditions must continue to be met throughout the prescribed period, and a failure to do so may give rise to an obligation to repay the benefit of the reduced rate. That obligation falls on the person for whom the works were carried out. A DISTINCT REGIME APPLIES TO RENOVATION: a separate reduced rate exists for works on a dwelling that is at least ten years old, subject to its own conditions which are not those set out above. The two regimes are not interchangeable and a conclusion reached under one does not establish the position under the other. ALLOCATION OF RESPONSIBILITY: entitlement to the reduced rate belongs to the person for whom the works are carried out, who must satisfy and continue to satisfy the conditions. The company is in principle answerable for the correct application of the rate, save where it holds the client's declaration and was unaware that it was incorrect. NO TAX ADVICE IS GIVEN: the company does not provide tax or accountancy advice, which are reserved to professionals registered with the ITAA, nor legal advice, which is reserved to the Bar. Statements on this site describe rules of general application so that a client knows which questions arise, and the position of any particular project is to be confirmed by the client's own adviser.
